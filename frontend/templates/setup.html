Before you process manga

Fox Reader is a tool for manga you own or hold rights to process — your own scans, licensed volumes, public-domain works, or works whose rights holders permit it. The tool grants you no rights in anyone else's manga.

  • Do not redistribute what is not yours. Fox Reader is not for stripping, re-lettering, or redistributing pages you have no right to copy — including uploading others' pages to shared endpoints, public Colab runtimes, or any service that stores them.
  • Endpoints you configure are yours to vouch for. Point /translate/custom — or any endpoint you add — only at a service you host or are permitted to use. Choosing the destination is choosing who receives your pages and text.
  • Manga109-s results carry conditions. If you publish results derived from Manga109-s material: attribute Manga109-s, never redistribute the dataset, never sell images together with results, and publish at most 20% of whole pages per volume.
  • None of this is enforced by the app. Fox Reader does not inspect your pages, count what you publish, or verify ownership, and it never phones home. Compliance is yours.
